# 🧨 Rate My Patakha — Simple User Guide

Welcome to **Rate My Patakha**! Create your Diwali Cracker Card, frame your Photo or Rangoli, challenge friends in 1v1 Battles, and climb the live Leaderboard.

---

## ⚡ 1. Quick Start: Get Your Cracker Card in 5 Seconds

1. Open the home page and select the **"🧨 Cracker Card"** tab.
2. Type your **@username** (for example, `@rohan`) or paste your profile link, then click **"Generate My Cracker Card"**.
3. **What Happens Next**:
   - If you already created a card earlier, we load your saved card and your current live rank.
   - If you are new, you get matched with a Patakha Clan and appear on the live Leaderboard right away.
4. **Download & Share**:
   - Click **"Download Poster"** to save a high-quality PNG image of your card.
   - Click **"Copy Link"** or **"Share Poster + Link"** to send your voting link (`/?u=yourname`) to friends on WhatsApp or X (`x.com`) so they can upvote you in 1 click!

---

## 🎁 2. Bonus Rule: Upvote 3 Other Users = +1 Bonus Vote for You!

Want to increase your own vote count even faster?

- **How It Works**: Every time you upvote **3 other users** on the Leaderboard or Clan list (3, 6, 9, 12...), **your own score automatically increases by +1 vote in real time**!
- You can track your progress (`0/3` → `1/3` → `2/3` → `+1 Bonus!`) right in the top bar, on the Cracker Card studio, and at the top of the Leaderboard.

---

## 📸 3. Frame Your Photo or Rangoli (`Photo / Rangoli Frame` Tab)

1. Click the **"📷 Photo / Rangoli Frame"** tab.
2. Click **"Upload Photo / Rangoli"** and choose any photo from your phone or computer.
3. Pick from **4 Festive Frames**:
   - **🪵 Engraved Oak Wood Plaque**
   - **🏛️ Crimson Palace Jharokha Arch**
   - **🌼 Marigold & Clay Diya Ring**
   - **✨ Golden Cream Polaroid**
4. **Important Privacy Note — Download First**:
   - For your privacy, your uploaded photo stays 100% on your device and is **never uploaded to our servers**.
   - Because of this, your personal photo will not appear inside the voting link. Always click **"1. Download Poster"** first, and share that downloaded photo along with your voting link!

---

## ⚔️ 4. Play a 1v1 Battle With Friends (`1v1 Battle` Tab)

1. Click **"⚔️ 1v1 Battle"** at the top of the page (or click the **`1v1`** button next to any user on the Leaderboard).
2. Type your **@username** and your friend's **@username** (or tap a Quick Pick Rival button).
3. Choose how many hours the battle should last (**1 to 48 hours**).
4. Click **"Download 1v1 VS Poster PNG"** and **"Copy @Tag Challenge Text"** to tag your friend on WhatsApp or X (`x.com`).
5. As soon as the countdown timer finishes, the person with the most votes is crowned the **Official 1v1 Battle Winner**!

---

## 🏆 5. Live Leaderboard & Grand Finale

- **Real-Time Updates**: All vote numbers, clan totals, and rankings update live on every screen.
- **1-Week Grand Finale**: While 1v1 Battles announce winners as soon as their timer ends, the **Overall #1 Leaderboard & Clan Winners will be announced in 1 week**!
