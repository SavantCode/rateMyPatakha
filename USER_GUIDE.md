# 🧨 Rate My Patakha — Official User & Creator Manual

Welcome to **Rate My Patakha**, an interactive Diwali Cracker Card Studio, Handcrafted Wooden & Rajasthani Photo/Rangoli Frame Generator, Clan Roster Browser, and Real-Time 1v1 Rivalry Battle Arena!

---

## ⚡ 1. Quick Start: Generate Your Cracker Card (In 5 Seconds)

1. Open the home page (`/`) and select the **"🧨 Cracker Card"** tab.
2. Type your **@username** (e.g., `@rohan`) or paste your profile URL (e.g., `https://rohan.substack.com`).
3. **Instant Database Verification**:
   - If your username has **already created a card**, a green badge appears (`"✅ @username already made a card"`), loading your locked card and live rank!
   - If you are new, you are deterministically categorized into one of our Patakha Clans, locked in MongoDB, and ranked on the live leaderboard.
4. **Unlimited Downloads & Sharing**:
   - Tap **"Download Poster"** as many times as you like to save a **1080×1350 HD Golden Cream Polaroid PNG**.
   - Tap **"Share Poster + Link"** on mobile to share both the PNG poster and your personal voting link (`/?u=yourname`) to WhatsApp, Instagram, or X!

---

## 📸 2. Upload Your Photo, Rangoli, or Festive Snap (`Photo / Rangoli Frame` Tab)

Want to frame your own **selfie, Rangoli artwork, glowing Diyas, or family celebration photo** while competing on the Patakha Leaderboard?

1. Switch to the **"📷 Photo / Rangoli Frame"** tab.
2. Tap **"Upload Photo / Rangoli"** to select any photograph from your phone or computer.
3. **Choose from 4 Handcrafted Frames (`1. Frame Style`)**:
   - **🪵 Engraved Oak Wood Plaque**: Realistic timber grain, 3D beveled edges, laser-engraved hanging lanterns, and a wood-burned Lotus Diya.
   - **🏛️ Crimson Palace Jharokha Arch**: Scalloped Rajasthani arch, jasmine/marigold garlands, and 3 colorful Diyas.
   - **🌼 Marigold & Clay Diya Ring**: Circular folk geometric ring (`▲ • ▲ •`), marigold toran, and terracotta Diya.
   - **✨ Golden Cream Polaroid**: Warm cream editorial card with double gold foil borders.
4. **⚠️ Important Note: Please Download First Before Sharing Your Voting Link!**
   - For your privacy, **your uploaded photo or Rangoli is processed 100% inside your browser and is NEVER uploaded to our servers**.
   - Because of this, **your personal photo will NOT be shown when someone opens your shareable voting link (`/?u=yourname`)**—the link displays your Patakha Cracker Card for voting.
   - **Always click "1. Download Poster" first** (you can download unlimited times anytime!) and share that downloaded PNG image alongside your voting link on WhatsApp, Instagram, or X!

## 🧭 3. Browse Other Users' Clans & Member Rosters (`Browse Clans`)

Curious which users got **Murga Bomb**, **Atom Bomb**, **Rocket**, or **Chakri**?

1. Click **"🧭 Browse Clans"** in the top switcher (or scroll to **Explore Patakha Clans & Member Rosters**).
2. Swipe through the Clan Cards or switch to **Grid View** to see how many members and total votes each clan has.
3. **Interactive Clan Roster Spotlight**:
   - Tap any Clan Card to open its **Clan Roster Spotlight** right below the cards!
   - Browse the top members inside that clan, click **👁️ View** to inspect their card, click **⚔️ 1v1** to challenge any clan member to a battle, or **🔥 Upvote** them directly!

---

## ⚔️ 4. How to Play a 1v1 Battle by Tagging Your Friends (`/?vs=user1,user2&days=N`)

Want to challenge a friend, creator, or colleague to a multi-day Diwali voting showdown?

1. **Enter the Battle Arena**:
   - Tap **"⚔️ 1v1 Battle"** at the top of the page (or tap **`⚔️ 1v1`** next to any user in the Clan Roster or Leaderboard).
2. **Set Your Custom Battle Duration (Days)**:
   - Use the **Battle Duration Bar** to pick **1, 3, 5, 7, 14 Days**, or type **any custom number of days (`1–90 Days`)**!
   - A live **Countdown Timer (`⏳ 4d 23h 59m 12s left`)** ticks down in real time.
3. **Tag Your Rival on Social Media**:
   - Click **"Download 1v1 VS Poster PNG"** to save the side-by-side 1080×1350 matchup poster.
   - Click **"Copy @Tag Challenge Text"**, **"Tag on WhatsApp"**, or **"Tag @rival on X"** and tag `@yourfriend` so both of your communities can vote before the countdown ends!

## 🚀 5. Automatic Hourly Momentum Boost & Fair-Play Security

- **⚡ Hourly Community Momentum Boost**:
  - If any registered user goes more than **1 hour** without receiving an upvote, the Arena Momentum Engine automatically awards **+1 upvote per elapsed idle hour** so every creator feels continuous engagement when they check back.
- **🔒 Anti-Tampering & Deduplicated Voting**:
  - Once a username registers their Patakha Card, their clan is locked in MongoDB (`$setOnInsert`) so visitors cannot overwrite it, and upvotes are protected via SHA-256 IP + Browser fingerprinting.
